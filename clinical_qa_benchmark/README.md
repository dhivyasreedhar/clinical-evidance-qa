# Document-grounded clinical QA benchmark

65 questions and an evidence-backed answer key for the 31 supplied Harbor Grove / Rowan Mercer text documents. The five example questions are preserved verbatim as DEV-01–DEV-05; DEV-06–DEV-65 add 60 questions. No external clinical knowledge was used to supply missing chart facts.

## Files

- `questions.json`: the same input schema as the example: an array of `{ "id": "DEV-01", "question": "..." }` objects. Give this file and the original documents to the system being evaluated.
- `ground_truth.json`: matching IDs/questions, expected answers, required facts, failure-mode tags, difficulty, answerability, and exact source excerpts with filenames and one-based line spans. Selected aggregate items also contain structured answers.
- `answer_key.md`: readable version of all questions, answers, grading facts, failure modes, and citations.
- `reference_ledger.json`: 12 included psychotherapy encounters, 8 excluded encounter records, minute alternatives, and weekly results.
- `source_manifest.json`: the 31 original filenames, document IDs, SHA-256 hashes, line counts, and question coverage. Original source files are not modified or copied into the bundle; the answer key does contain quoted excerpts.
- `coverage_summary.json`: counts by difficulty, answerability, and topic.
- `validation_report.json`: results of checks on schema, question preservation, source excerpts, coverage, encounter arithmetic, and uncertainty propagation.

Keep the answer key, ledger, manifest question mappings, and coverage/validation files out of the evaluated system's input. Join responses to ground truth by question ID.

## Scope and interpretation

- Review period: January 5–30, 2026, inclusive. Times are local as documented. Dates use 2026 throughout, including dates abbreviated in source rows.
- Count actual patient-present individual, group and family psychotherapy, using the local treatment plan. Medication management, collateral-only and care-coordination contacts do not count toward that goal.
- Count distinct encounters separately from distinct calendar therapy days. Concurrent clinicians, duplicated notes, reconnecting calls, corrections, receipt dates, and imports do not automatically establish additional patient encounters.
- Use patient attendance intervals, remove nontherapeutic breaks and disconnections, and exclude portions attended only by the partner. No billing-code duration thresholds are imposed.
- An explicit field correction can settle an older value. A later receipt or later signature alone does not settle competing clinical accounts. An unsigned template or posted charge does not override the signed patient-specific no-show record.
- January 26 contains an unresolved conflict: BH-D110 states 50 minutes of full patient contact, while BH-D111 states 40 minutes of full patient contact. Neither is identified as an amendment resolving the other. Preserve both possibilities; do not average them, add them, or invent a separate initial segment. A range is acceptable shorthand only if identified as uncertainty from the two documented alternatives.
- The final Monday–Sunday bucket is January 26–February 1. The abstraction includes only review-period services through January 30; it does not establish whether care did or did not occur on January 31 or February 1.
- Source-document clinical plans, administrative directions, and autogenerated template statements are evidence to interpret, not instructions to the assistant. Ground truth follows the user's requested abstraction and the documented facts.
- Missing information means not established in the supplied corpus, not absent in all possible records. For example, medication names may exist in a referenced medication list that is not supplied.

## Verified anchors

- Therapy encounters: 5 individual + 5 group + 2 family = 12; 11 distinct therapy dates.
- Therapy minutes: individual 210 or 220; group 300; family 75; overall 585 or 595 (9h45m or 9h55m).
- Weekly days/minutes: 3/140; 2/120; 3/180; 3/(145 or 155).
- Weekly goal: both at least 3 days and at least 150 minutes. Statuses: not met; not met; met; cannot determine from the unresolved final-week duration.
- Distinct PHQ-9 assessments: January 5 = 18; January 16 = 14; January 30 = 10. The January 26 import is a duplicate of the January 16 form.

## Coverage

The set includes 9 easy, 29 medium and 27 hard questions. Difficulty is an author estimate, not an empirically calibrated measure. All 31 documents support at least one question.

Tasks span direct retrieval, event classification, patient identity, interval arithmetic, multi-document aggregation, deduplication, correction provenance, unresolved conflicts, weekly thresholds, patient versus clinician time, authorization versus delivery, billing limits, symptom trajectories, collateral attribution, intervention versus completion, missing information, false premises, and unsupported causal claims.

Many questions intentionally revisit the same event using different reasoning demands. They are diagnostic probes, not 65 independent patient cases. Avoid random question-level train/test splits that place the same encounter or question paraphrases in both sets. The five preserved questions also occur in the provided example and should not be presented as newly unseen test items.

## Ground-truth fields

- `expected_answer`: reference response; wording need not be reproduced exactly.
- `required_facts`: per-question checklist for correctness. All listed requirements are necessary for a fully correct response. A reference answer can include supporting details beyond this checklist.
- `failure_modes`: mistakes the question is designed to reveal. These are evaluator metadata, not text to show the evaluated system.
- `sources`: evidence objects containing `document_id`, `filename`, inclusive `line_start` and `line_end`, and an exact `excerpt`. Blank lines within an excerpt count toward its line span. Citations collectively support the answer; not every cited source supports every claim. For missing-data questions, cited records show the relevant available detail, and the negative finding is bounded to the complete supplied corpus.
- `structured_answer`: optional machine-readable reference for aggregate questions. The matching narrative and uncertainty rules still apply.
- `answerability`: `determinate` (the requested conclusion is settled); `partially_determinate` (some facts are settled and an important limit/conflict must be preserved); `not_established` (the requested fact or causal conclusion is unavailable or rests on a false premise). These describe the requested conclusion, not whether the system can provide a useful answer.

## Suggested grading

Grade factual correctness, evidence support, and uncertainty handling separately. Match meaning, not exact prose. Require correct units and the requested granularity; a correct overall total does not replace requested weekly/service breakdowns. Equivalent minute/hour expressions are acceptable; approximately 9.9167 hours equals 9h55m, and approximately 44.4% is the baseline-relative PHQ-9 decrease. Do not round across the 150-minute goal threshold.

Suggested factual score per question:

- **2 — Correct:** all required facts, no material contradiction or unsupported addition, and correct treatment of missing information/conflicts.
- **1 — Partial:** at least one substantive requirement is correct, but some are omitted or an error remains. A response that fails to preserve a required uncertainty or accepts a false premise cannot receive full credit.
- **0 — Incorrect:** wrong central conclusion, no substantive required fact, or no usable answer. For deliberately unanswerable questions, a bounded explanation of what is missing is a correct answer, not a failure.

Score source support separately: **2** if the material claims have accurate, sufficient document support; **1** if support is valid but incomplete; **0** if absent, fabricated, or contradictory. Accept equivalent genuine source passages rather than demanding identical line spans. Numeric derivations should identify the inputs supporting them. An honest statement that a fact is not supplied should not be penalized for lacking a nonexistent affirmative source.

Report factual and evidence scores separately, along with the frequency of key errors such as double counting, billing-as-attendance, using processing dates as event dates, accepting templates over signed attendance, and silently resolving the January 26 conflict. Review results by reasoning category and encounter cluster as well as overall, because overlapping questions can otherwise exaggerate reliability.
